I'm Fouad El Sayed, a Senior Mud Logger at Petro services GmbH (Egypt branch), based in Qalyobia, Cairo, Egypt. I hold a BSc in Geophysics from Ain Shams University with a cumulative grade of Very Good (B+).

With 3+ years of hands-on well-site experience, I specialize in monitoring drilling fluids and providing crucial geological insights for oil and gas exploration projects. I'm proficient in data collection, analysis, and reporting to ensure wellbore stability and drilling efficiency.

I progressed from Mud Logger to Senior Mud Logger at Petro services GmbH, and trained with industry leaders including Rashpetco (Shell-JV), Khalda Petroleum Company, and Schlumberger.

I'm actively seeking new opportunities in the energy industry, collaborating with drilling teams to optimize operations, maintain safety standards, and drive success.
